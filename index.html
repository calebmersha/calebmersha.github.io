Privacy

Privacy Policy

Information about how FixIT applications handle user data.

FixIT IT Solution respects your privacy. Our applications are designed to provide useful functionality while minimizing the collection of personal information.

Information Collection

Subnetify does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the core subnet calculation features.

IP Address Information

IP addresses entered into Subnetify are processed locally on the device for calculation purposes. The application does not send the entered IP address to a FixIT server for processing.

Advertising

Subnetify may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising services may collect or use certain information according to Google's applicable policies and settings.

Information Sharing

FixIT IT Solution does not sell users' personal information. Information handled by third-party services, such as advertising providers, is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Internet Access

Internet access may be required for advertising and related third-party services. The main subnet calculation functionality is designed to work locally on the device.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when application functionality or applicable requirements change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact FixIT IT Solution through the contact information provided on this website.
